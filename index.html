Settings & prediction tuning

How prediction works. The cycle length is the average of your last three in-range cycles (falling back to the default). The fertile window spans the five days before ovulation through the day after. Ovulation is taken from, in priority order, a manual mark, your basal-temperature shift, a wearable reading, a positive OPK, a fertility-monitor peak, then full ferning. The temperature shift is detected with a coverline: a run of low temperatures followed by a sustained rise of at least 0.1 °C.

All data stays in this browser only — nothing is uploaded. This is a planning aid, not a contraceptive method or medical advice.
